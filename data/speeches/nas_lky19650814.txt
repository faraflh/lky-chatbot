MR. LEE KUAN YEW, TO FOUR FOREIGN CORRESPONDENTS ON
AUGUST 14,1965, AT THE STUDIOS OF TELEVISION SINGAPURA.

by four correspondents. My name is Richard Croll from Channel 7 in Sydney.
My name is Sam Lipski from the Australian Bulletin; Gavin Young, I am
from the London Observer.
