# Data rights and provenance

This project prioritises primary source material with a recorded provenance URL. Each source should have a title, date, document type, origin URL, and rights/approval status in the source manifest.

The memoirs listed in `data/source_manifest.csv` are copyrighted books. Their full PDFs and extracted full text are deliberately excluded from the public repository. Before a source is placed in a public Dify knowledge base, verify that the intended use and redistribution are permitted.

For archival records, retain a link to the original archive record and record the date on which the material was accessed. Do not treat online availability as permission to redistribute a full work.
